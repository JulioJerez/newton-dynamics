Camera & Navigation

The editor uses a flyby-style camera with pitch and yaw rotation, pan, and zoom controlled entirely by the mouse. No keyboard shortcuts are needed for navigation.

Flyby Camera

The default camera (ndEditorCameraFlyby) stores a position in local space and two rotation angles — pitch (up/down tilt) and yaw (left/right rotation). Each frame these are combined into a view matrix and applied to the scene light direction as well.

Pitch is clamped to ±80° to prevent gimbal singularities. Yaw wraps continuously.

Rotation rates: Yaw and pitch both default to 0.2 × 60 = 12 deg/s per pixel of mouse movement. These values are set at construction time in ndEditorCameraFlyby.

Mouse Controls

All viewport interaction fires only when the cursor is not captured by an ImGui panel.

🖱️ Left

Orbit (rotate)

Drag left/right to yaw the camera around the scene. Drag up/down to pitch. The first click also fires a mesh/bone selection ray-cast.

🖱️ Right

Pan

Drag left/right to slide the view along Z. Drag up/down to slide along Y. Pan speed defaults to 10 units/second × timestep.

🖱️ Middle

Zoom

Drag up to zoom in, drag down to zoom out. Adjusts the camera's orthographic/perspective zoom factor, clamped between 0.01 and 100.

🖱️ Scroll

Not used for zoom

Mouse scroll is forwarded to ImGui for scrolling panels. Use Middle-drag to zoom the viewport.

Preset Views

Use the camera mode dropdown in the Main Toolbar to snap to an orthogonal viewpoint. The camera position is preserved; only yaw/pitch change.

←
Free Camera

No rotation preset. Use mouse to navigate freely.

↑
Back View

Yaw = 0°, Pitch = 0°. Looking along +Z.

↓
Front View

Yaw = 180°, Pitch = 0°. Looking along −Z.

→
Left Side View

Yaw = 90°, Pitch = 0°. Looking along −X.

←
Right Side View

Yaw = −90°, Pitch = 0°. Looking along +X.

Tip: After switching to a preset view, you can still orbit with Left-drag to examine from a nearby angle. Switch back to Free Camera if you want unconstrained navigation.

Projection Mode

The Perspective / Orthographic button in the toolbar toggles the camera projection. The button label always shows the current mode; clicking it switches.

ModeWhen to use
PerspectiveGeneral scene exploration and visual inspection. Default.
OrthographicPrecise alignment, measuring relative sizes, top-down or side-on technical views. Combined with a preset view for best results.

The zoom factor (changed via Middle-drag) controls the orthographic view width in orthographic mode and acts as a field-of-view scale in perspective mode.

Click Selection in the Viewport

A ray-cast fires on the first Left-click frame (not while dragging). The behaviour depends on the Show mesh skeleton option in the Options menu:

ModeWhat gets selected
Skeleton mode ONThe nearest bone node whose bone-to-tip segment is within 0.1 world-units of the ray. Misses are ignored.
Skeleton mode OFFThe nearest mesh geometry that the ray intersects. The entire mesh triangle set is tested using RayCast.

If Lock Selection is active in the toolbar, viewport clicks do not change the selection at all.

Selection is also gated by the current sub-selection mode. For example, when you're in loop joint mode, clicking a different node sets it as the sub-selection target rather than the main selection.
